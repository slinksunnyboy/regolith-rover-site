How to play

  • Jump over craters, mines and rocks.
  • Fire shoots forward and up at the same time: blast rocks, boulders and tanks ahead, and craft overhead.
  • Hold Slow or Fast to change speed; let go to return to normal speed.
  • Reach checkpoints E, J, O, T and Z faster than the average time for a bonus.

Game controllers and keyboards work too: arrows or A/D to change speed, Up/W/Space to jump, Z/J/Return to fire, Esc or P to pause, M to mute.

Common questions

I bought a world or pack on another device. How do I get it here?
Open Worlds and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account. Purchases carry across iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Do purchases make the game easier?
No. Worlds and vehicles change how the game looks, never how it plays, so every score is earned the same way.

There's no sound.
Check the speaker button in the game and the silent switch on your iPhone; the game follows it.

Does the game collect my data?
No. See the privacy policy.
